Components Information

1. Arduino Uno

Arduino Uno is the main controller of the robotic car. It processes sensor data and controls the motors.

2. L298N Motor Driver

It controls the speed and direction of the DC motors.

3. Ultrasonic Sensor (HC-SR04)

It detects obstacles by measuring the distance between the robot and nearby objects.

4. DC Motors

These motors help the robotic car move forward, backward, and turn.

5. 7.4V Battery

It provides electrical power to the robotic car.

6. Robot Chassis

It provides the physical structure for mounting all components.

7. Breadboard

It helps in making temporary electrical connections.

8. Jumper Wires

They connect different electronic components.

9. ON/OFF Switch

It is used to turn the robotic car ON and OFF.
